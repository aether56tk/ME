Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This notice explains how ClinicCraft handles information when you visit this website or submit a project enquiry. ClinicCraft provides document formatting, presentation formatting and editable-template services. This page is a practical notice, not a guarantee of legal compliance.

1. Information we collect

If you submit the enquiry form, we collect only the fields shown there: your name, email address, requested service and project message. Please do not submit patient-identifiable information, health records, passwords, payment-card details or confidential third-party information.

2. Why we use it

We use enquiry details to understand your request, reply to you, discuss scope and pricing, and follow up on the project. We do not intentionally collect more information than is needed for these purposes, and we do not use the form to collect clinical records.

3. Where it is processed

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The enquiry form sends the fields you submit to a Supabase database service. The page loads the Supabase JavaScript library from jsDelivr. These providers may process technical request data under their own terms and privacy notices. The site currently does not intentionally include advertising pixels, analytics tags, social-media embeds or video embeds.

4. Sharing

We do not sell enquiry information. Data may be processed by the hosting, database and library-delivery providers needed to operate the site, or disclosed where required by law. We do not promise that internet transmission is risk-free.

5. Retention and security

Enquiry details should be kept only as long as needed to respond, manage a project, resolve a dispute or meet a legal requirement. We aim to restrict access to the database and avoid collecting sensitive information. No website or database can be guaranteed completely secure. A documented deletion schedule and operational deletion process should be maintained by the site operator.

6. Your requests

For a request to access, correct or delete enquiry information, or to raise a privacy concern, email cliniccraft.studio@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request”. We may need enough information to identify the relevant enquiry, but please do not send passwords or identity documents unless specifically necessary and requested through a safe process. We will review and respond as applicable law requires.

7. Children and sensitive information

This website is intended for people seeking document and presentation services, not for children to submit personal information. Do not upload or enter patient data, special-category information or information about another person without appropriate authority.

8. Changes and contact

This notice may be updated as the website changes. Contact: cliniccraft.studio@gmail.com. ClinicCraft currently publishes no public street address or registered-entity details on this website; those details should be added if applicable before accepting paid work at scale.

India privacy note: the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Rules, 2025 have phased commencement provisions. This notice is a starting point, not a legal opinion or certification of compliance.
