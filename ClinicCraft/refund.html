Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Always obtain a written quote and confirm the scope, price, timeline, payment terms and included revisions by email before paying. This policy is a practical draft and must be aligned with the actual payment process and applicable consumer law.

Before work starts

If you cancel before work has started, contact us at cliniccraft.studio@gmail.com. Any refund will be assessed against payments received, any clearly disclosed non-recoverable costs, and applicable law.

After work starts

If work has started, the amount refundable may depend on the work completed and costs already incurred. We will explain the calculation in writing. We will not treat this policy as removing any mandatory rights you may have under applicable law.

Problems with a delivered file

If a delivered file does not match the agreed written scope, email us within 7 calendar days of delivery, describe the issue and attach a non-confidential example if possible. We will review it and, where appropriate, correct the agreed-scope issue. Additional work or a change of brief may require a new quote. The 7-day contact window does not limit statutory rights.

Non-refundable situations

We may decline a refund for a change of mind after substantial work has been completed, customer-provided source material that is incorrect or unusable, or requests outside the agreed scope, to the extent permitted by applicable law. This does not affect non-waivable consumer rights.

Refund processing

If a refund is approved, we will confirm the amount and method by email and use the original payment method where practical and lawful. Processing times can depend on the payment provider. Do not send card numbers, passwords or OTPs by email.

Contact

Email cliniccraft.studio@gmail.com with subject “Refund request” and include your quote or project reference if available.
