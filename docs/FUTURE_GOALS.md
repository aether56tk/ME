## Decision filter

Before starting a new goal, ask:
1. Does it support a current priority or a meaningful long-term direction?
2. What is the smallest useful next action?
3. What will I pause so I do not overload myself?
4. How will I know whether it is working?
5. Is it safe, ethical, and realistic with my current responsibilities?
