## Study loop

1. **Preview:** read the learning objectives and scan the unit.
2. **Understand:** learn one small concept in plain language.
3. **Recall:** close the notes and explain it without looking.
4. **Apply:** answer a 2-mark, 5-mark, and/or 10-mark question.
5. **Check:** correct errors and record one thing to revisit.

Use short sessions and visible next actions. On low-focus days, do five minutes or answer three recall questions rather than abandoning the day.

## Clinical skills ladder

Track practice and feedback; do not mark a skill competent solely because it has been read.

- [ ] Case history: concerns, symptoms, observations, and relevant history
- [ ] Otoscopy: indications, safety, and supervised procedure
- [ ] Pure-tone audiometry: procedure, reliability, and interpretation
- [ ] Speech audiometry: SRT, SDT, SIS/WRS, MCL, and UCL concepts
- [ ] Masking: rationale and basic decision-making
- [ ] Immittance: tympanometry and acoustic reflex concepts
- [ ] OAE and ABR: purpose, limitations, and interpretation basics
- [ ] Speech sound assessment and phonetic transcription
- [ ] Language sample, goal writing, and therapy documentation
- [ ] Rehabilitation counselling and caregiver communication

## Exam answer bank

For each unit, build three levels:

- **2 marks:** definition, key features, short distinction, or list
- **5 marks:** structured explanation with headings and examples
- **10 marks:** introduction, classification/mechanism, assessment, management, examples, and conclusion as appropriate

Map previous-year questions to units. Verify answers against prescribed textbooks, lecture notes, and current clinical guidance.
