## Exam preparation loop

For each unit:
1. **Map:** list headings and match previous questions.
2. **Understand:** study one small section.
3. **Recall:** close notes and explain it aloud or write key points.
4. **Practise:** answer a 2-mark, 5-mark, and 10-mark question where applicable.
5. **Repair:** mark gaps and revisit them later.
6. **Log:** record what was actually completed.

Suggested study block:
- 2 minutes: choose the exact topic.
- 15 minutes: learn or revise.
- 5 minutes: recall without looking.
- 3 minutes: mark gaps and choose the next action.

If starting feels impossible, do only the first two minutes. Continuing is optional.
