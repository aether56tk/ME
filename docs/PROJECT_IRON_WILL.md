# Project Iron Will

A long-term personal development commitment focused on becoming more capable, reliable, healthy, compassionate, and professionally skilled. The original horizon was 9 April 2025 to 30 July 2028; treat it as a direction, not a reason for guilt if plans change.

## Core pillars

1. **Character:** honesty, patience, accountability, and kindness.
2. **Education:** finish BASLP and rebuild fundamentals, including SSD.
3. **Clinical skill:** develop careful reasoning, communication, documentation, and supervised practical skills.
4. **Research and technology:** build useful projects and report evidence honestly.
5. **Career:** work toward medical-device cybersecurity, safety, privacy, and defensive security.
6. **Physical wellbeing:** build strength, endurance, mobility, recovery, and sustainable routines.
7. **Independence:** develop practical skills and, over time, contribute financially to family where feasible.
8. **Relationships:** build healthy friendships, boundaries, and respectful communication.

## Minimum viable day

When energy or focus is low, the goal is to keep the connection to the plan—not to force a heroic workload.

- [ ] Do one 5–15 minute study action
- [ ] Move, stretch, or walk within safe personal limits
- [ ] Eat, hydrate, and protect sleep as best as possible
- [ ] Complete one small responsibility
- [ ] Decide the next action for tomorrow

A missed day does not require punishment or doubling tomorrow's workload.

## Weekly scorecard

Record whether you made progress, not whether you were perfect.

| Area | Weekly evidence |
|---|---|
| BASLP | Topic revised, questions answered, or clinical concept clarified |
| SSD | One small revision session |
| Project | One documented improvement or decision |
| Fitness | Sustainable movement and recovery |
| Personal growth | One useful action, boundary, or reflection |
| Rest | A deliberate recovery choice |

## Guardrails

- Avoid extreme workouts, sleep deprivation, and all-or-nothing challenges.
- Increase training gradually and rest when unwell or injured.
- Keep goals flexible around exams and clinical responsibilities.
- Measure progress through consistency, learning, and behaviour—not comparison or revenge.
- Ask for support when stress feels unmanageable.

## Milestones

- [ ] Complete current academic requirements and backlog plan
- [ ] Maintain a record of supervised clinical learning
- [ ] Document project progress with tests and limitations
- [ ] Build a small defensive-security portfolio
- [ ] Establish a repeatable, sustainable fitness routine
- [ ] Review goals monthly and remove goals that no longer fit
