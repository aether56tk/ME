# Research Lab

A home for research questions, study plans, validation evidence, and project decisions. Keep claims proportional to the evidence.

## Research principles

- Start with a clear problem, target population, and intended use.
- Separate a concept, prototype, tested implementation, and validated result.
- Record methods, versions, assumptions, limitations, and decisions.
- Prefer reproducible analysis and transparent reporting.
- Obtain appropriate institutional/ethics approval before human-participant research.
- Use consented, de-identified data and follow institutional rules.
- Never commit patient-identifying information, case files, recordings, or confidential clinical material to a public repository.

## Portfolio

### HearSec
**Working direction:** An evidence-grounded cybersecurity and privacy risk assessment framework for connected hearing aids.

Focus areas to investigate: Bluetooth/BLE exposure, companion apps, fitting software, cloud services, authentication, access control, logging, update mechanisms, privacy, and AI-enabled features.

Next steps:
- [ ] Freeze and version the assessment framework
- [ ] Define control questions and evidence requirements
- [ ] Test unknown/not-rated responses and prevent unsupported conclusions
- [ ] Create synthetic examples that contain no real patient/device secrets
- [ ] Evaluate consistency, usability, and evidence traceability
- [ ] Seek qualified expert review before making validation claims

Repository: https://github.com/aether56tk/HearSec

### Vowel Space Area
**Working direction:** Calculate vowel-space measures from formant values for the four selected corner vowels.

Potential measures: VSA, perimeter, centroid, and formant concentration ratio (FCR), with formulas and input assumptions documented clearly.

Status note: **Parked until explicitly resumed. Do not modify the project unless I specifically ask.**

### TinniRelief
**Working direction:** Browser-based tinnitus rehabilitation concept.

Possible development areas: clear sound categories, safe-use information, session tracking, accessible interface, and transparent limitations. Do not present it as a medical treatment or claim effectiveness without appropriate evidence.

Repository: https://github.com/aether56tk/TinniRelief

### EPG-CP concept
**Working direction:** Explore an electropalatography-inspired research prototype for studying tongue–palate contact in cerebral palsy-related speech research.

Keep the tabletop demonstration prototype clearly separate from any proposed intraoral clinical device. A future intraoral system would require specialist engineering, biocompatibility, electrical safety, infection-control planning, ethics review, and clinical validation before human use.

## Research record template

For every project, maintain:
1. Question and rationale
2. Intended users and scope
3. Related work and source notes
4. Methods and version
5. Test data provenance and privacy checks
6. Results and limitations
7. Risks and ethics considerations
8. Next decision and date
