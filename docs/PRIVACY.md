# Privacy and Safety

This repository is public. Only publish information that would be okay for classmates, future employers, and strangers to read.

## Never commit
- Passwords, API keys, access tokens, private keys, or environment files
- Patient names, initials linked to identifiable details, contact details, dates of birth, photographs, reports, recordings, identifiable case histories
- Private health records, therapy notes, or sensitive family details
- College documents or research data you do not have permission to share
- Personal location routines or information that could compromise safety

## Clinical and research work
Use fictional examples or appropriately de-identified data only when permitted by institutional policy and applicable ethics approval. Removing a name alone may not make a case anonymous. Do not upload patient data to public repositories or unapproved AI services.

## Security project work
Use only systems and devices you own or are explicitly authorised to test. Document safe scope, evidence, and mitigations. Do not publish exploit details that expose real users or devices to harm.

## Public profile principle
Share goals, learning milestones, project links, and evidence of work. Keep intimate reflections and sensitive medical details in a private place.
