## How I choose what to learn next

Rate a possible task against:
- **Relevance:** does it support my degree or long-term direction?
- **Urgency:** is there a real deadline?
- **Leverage:** will it help several other goals?
- **Energy:** can I start it with the energy I have today?

During exam periods, urgency and degree relevance usually win. Curiosity projects remain parked in a backlog rather than becoming extra obligations.
