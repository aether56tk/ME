# Research and Software Portfolio

Keep project claims accurate. A concept, prototype, tested feature, expert-reviewed framework, and clinically validated product are different stages.

## HearSec
**Direction:** evidence-grounded cybersecurity and privacy risk assessment for connected hearing aids.

Potential areas to document:
- Bluetooth/BLE exposure and pairing
- Companion applications and permissions
- Authentication and access control
- Logging, cloud dependencies, and data handling
- Threat model, evidence sources, uncertainty, and mitigations
- Expert review and validation plan

**Next milestone:** audit the current repository, label implemented versus planned controls, and add reproducible tests and explicit limitations.

Repository: https://github.com/aether56tk/HearSec

Potential areas:

- Tests, documentation, and feedback-driven improvements

**Next milestone:** list existing features, missing features, tests, and what requires clinical review.

Repository: https://github.com/aether56tk/TinniRelief

## Vowel Space Area
**Direction:** calculate acoustic measures from formant values for the four corner vowels.

Possible measures include VSA, perimeter, centroid, and formant centralisation metrics, with formulas and assumptions documented.

**Next milestone:** audit formula implementation and input validation; add known-value tests and clear units. Do not change the project without a deliberate decision and backup.

Repository: https://github.com/aether56tk/vowel-space-area

## Project definition template

For each new project, answer:
1. Who is it for?
2. What specific problem does it solve?
3. What is the smallest useful version?
4. What evidence would show it works?
5. What could go wrong, and what are the limitations?
6. What data is collected, and how is privacy protected?
7. What is the next action small enough to do this week?
